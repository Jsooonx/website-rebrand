# Forma Common — Image Generation Prompts

All images were generated with the built-in ImageGen workflow as original
fictional portfolio material. Shared direction: contemporary design-portfolio
photography, hard architectural light, off-white and charcoal foundations,
controlled cobalt accents, precise material detail, no real logos, and no
watermarks.

## Work

### Brewline

Premium editorial product photograph of one sculptural slim aluminum canned
coffee and a folded cobalt paper sleeve on brushed stainless steel. Pale
warm-gray studio sweep, hard directional morning light, landscape crop,
original abstract line-and-block graphics, and the word `BREWLINE` once.

### Sore Ceramics

Three asymmetrical ceramic vessels on modular cobalt display plinths in a deep
warm-gray studio. Vertical crop, slightly low camera, hard side light, satin
glaze and unglazed clay, quiet gallery mood, and no readable text.

### Luma Pantry

Two glass jars, a matte carton, and graphic labels arranged on a translucent
acrylic shelf. Pale pistachio background, bright hard light, geometric identity
symbols, chrome details, landscape crop, and the word `LUMA` once.

### Sunday Social

A cobalt sidewalk sign, silver cafe chair, and folded off-white poster against
a minimal contemporary concrete facade. Bright midday light, bold cropped
geometry, restrained spatial-branding scene, and `SUNDAY SOCIAL` once.

### Kasa Stay

A cobalt room key tag, off-white guest card, brushed-steel tray, and sculptural
lamp in a minimal modern hotel interior. Vertical editorial crop, controlled
warm-to-cool dusk light, abstract K-form identity, and no readable text.

### Field Notes

Two frosted-glass botanical skincare bottles, a folded carton, and one
sculptural leaf on a stainless-steel base. Wide landscape crop, scientific but
human art direction, crisp hard light, and `FIELD NOTES` once.

### Teras Studio

A large modular campaign poster and cobalt wayfinding panel on a contemporary
concrete cultural building. Bright overcast daylight, landscape architectural
crop, tiny acid-green accent, and the word `TERAS` once.

### Nusa Objects

A sculptural chrome desk object, cobalt storage box, and off-white product
booklet arranged on a mirror against a charcoal studio sweep. Vertical crop,
hard spotlight, controlled reflections, slightly futuristic gallery mood, and
`NUSA OBJECTS` once.
